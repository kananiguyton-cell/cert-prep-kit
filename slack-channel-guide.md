# #claudecode_certprepkit — Channel Guide

Our hub for prepping three certs — **Tableau Next Consultant**, **Marketing Cloud Next
Consultant**, and **Data 360 Consultant**. Two things happen here:

1. **Log your progress** through the pinned **Cert Prep progress** workflow (📊). It's a quick
   form — exam, status, sections done, practice %, hardest sections, a tip. That's how the team
   tracker stays current.
2. **Talk shop** — post tips, resources, and "I'm stuck on X" freely. This is where the human
   signal lives.

## Log your progress (the workflow)

Click the pinned **📊 Cert Prep progress** workflow whenever you:
- finish a study session or a practice round, or
- sit the exam (pass or fail — both matter).

Fill the short form. You don't need to post anything else for tracking — the workflow handles it.

## Talk shop (free-form posts)

Post naturally. Light conventions keep it scannable:

**❓ Hard topic** — when something isn't clicking mid-study
```
❓ HARD TOPIC: MC Next — can't keep segment-triggered vs. record-triggered flow scenarios straight
```

**🔗 Resource** — when you find something that actually helped
```
🔗 RESOURCE: <link> — best explanation of BU↔Data Space isolation I've found
```

**🎉 Result** — celebrate a pass or share what a fail taught you (also log it in the workflow)
```
🎉 Data 360 — PASS! hardest: unification + activations. Solution Positioning is advisory framing, don't skip it.
```

## How to study

Study in **Claude Code** with the `cert-prep` skill — say "help me study for Data 360" (or any of
the three). You get product-aware 80/20 plans, scenario practice questions, live research on the
newest enablement, and topic tutoring — at full quality. Then log your progress via the workflow.

## Ground rules
- Don't share actual exam questions — section names and topic areas only (certification agreement
  applies). The skill generates original practice scenarios; never paste live exam content anywhere.
- First names or initials are fine.
- New joiner? Pin points you to the workflow + this guide. Ask for the `cert-prep` skill to study.
